DKcast

by Bolgaç Gülen

Temperature, with the range it lands in 8 days out of 10

Sunshine (minutes per hour) and chance of rain

How good is it?

13%less temperature error than the best public forecast
16%less error on sunshine and on rain
16%less wind error than the best public model
28%less temperature error than yr.no's own forecast, corrected

Tested once on 1 January to 18 October 2026, hours the model never saw, at DMI stations, against 12 public weather models (ECMWF IFS and AIFS, DWD ICON, DMI HARMONIE, MET Norway, UK Met Office, NOAA GFS and others), each given its own correction per station. DKcast reads all of them at once and learns, station by station, which to trust in which weather.

Forecast issued for , hours in Danish time. Inputs: public forecasts via Open-Meteo; truth: DMI open data. Method and full study: Denmark wind and weather study. Code: github.com/bolgacg/dk-wind-blend.
