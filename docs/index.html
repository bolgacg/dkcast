How good is it?

11%less wind error than the best public forecast
26%less temperature error than yr.no's forecast, in its best version

Tested once on 1 January to 18 October 2026, on hours the model never saw, at DMI stations, against 12 public weather models, each in its best version. The best public forecast is the average of all of them, each in its best version; against the best single model DKcast's lead is 15 to 22%. DKcast reads all of them at once and learns, station by station, which to trust in which weather.

About these forecasts

The forecasts are for DMI's weather stations, not for towns. Search a town and the page picks the nearest station; a station a few kilometres away usually has the same weather, but coasts and hills can differ. DKcast started as an energy project, forecasting Danish wind power for the electricity market; this weather site is a fun offshoot of the same model.

Inputs: public forecasts via Open-Meteo. Truth: DMI open data. Hours in Danish time. Method and full study: Denmark wind and weather study. Code: github.com/bolgacg/dk-wind-blend.
